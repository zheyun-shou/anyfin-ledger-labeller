Step 6 · Distil into the ML model · planned

Haiku teaches the small model

  • Collect confident labels (Haiku sure + ML agrees, plus spot-checked samples) as new training rows
  • Retrain TF-IDF + logistic regression on a schedule; re-tune the threshold on held-out customers
  • Each round the ML layer covers more rows, so fewer reach Haiku
  • Keep a fixed human-labelled test set, and never train on unverified outputs, so errors don't compound
